About Us

Your Home, Your Statement

Interstyle Home is the furniture and living division of the Interstyle group, bringing the world's most distinguished outdoor and indoor furniture collections to West Africa.

We curate only the finest pieces — from sculptural outdoor dining sets and Italian interior doors to statement indoor furniture and sumptuous soft furnishings.

From our showrooms in Lagos, Abuja and Accra, our expert consultants guide clients through a complete interior and exterior lifestyle offering.
