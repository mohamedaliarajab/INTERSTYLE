About Us

Crafting Spaces Since 2012

From inception, Interstyle Ceramics has adopted a research-based approach in selecting the finest manufacturers for its product range of tiles and sanitaryware — mainly manufacturers based in Europe, South America, and the GCC.

Incorporated in Nigeria in 2012, Interstyle introduced a diverse product range to meet the needs of customers who crave differentiation in positioning their projects through design peculiarity and consistent quality end products.

With a substantial turnover volume rate, four branches in Nigeria, a state-of-the-art showroom in Accra, Ghana, and a presence in Dakar, Senegal, Interstyle has the economies of scale to supply project customers with aggressively priced products in a timely manner.

Our mission is to raise the service standard in the industry and maintain a sustainable lead in the provision of home solutions in the tiles and sanitaryware sector.
