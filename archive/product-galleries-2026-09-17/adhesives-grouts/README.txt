TILE ADHESIVES & GROUTS — product gallery images
================================================

Save the images into this folder as 01.jpg .. 04.jpg:

  01.jpg   Grout colour chart — 40 named swatches with three-letter codes
           (Bright White BRW through Espresso ESP)
  02.jpg   Tiler setting black hexagon tiles into a notched adhesive bed
  03.jpg   STONETECH KlenzAll heavy-duty cleaner being sprayed on slate tile
  04.jpg   LATICRETE HYDRO BAN waterproofing membrane pail, pool application

FILENAMES DO NOT NEED TO BE EXACT. Save them under whatever names they
arrive with -- 1.png, 04.jpeg, image (2).webp -- as long as each has a
number in it. The gallery now retries .jpeg / .png / .webp automatically,
so they will show either way.

To compress them (recommended -- PNGs are often 10x larger than needed):

    python3 tools/optimise-images.py assets/img/products/adhesives-grouts
