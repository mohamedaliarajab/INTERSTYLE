Save the images into this folder as 01.jpg .. 06.jpg:

           black floor-standing bath mixer

           bath filler on a timber tray

           washbasin with chrome mixer

Any format is fine (PNG, WebP, JPEG) and any size — save them here and run:

    python3 tools/optimise-images.py assets/img/products/bathroom-fixtures

That renames anything like 1.png to 01.jpg, converts to real JPEG, resizes to
1800px max and strips transparency. Or just say the word and I'll run it.

Any slot without a file shows an "Image awaiting upload" placeholder rather
than a broken image, so you can add them one at a time.
